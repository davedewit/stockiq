## Faults

### 1. The plan cards on the dashboard promise features that don't exist (sure) → proposal #10

This is the "Upgrade Your Plan" section, where people pay (`dashboard.html` lines 1209–1245).

- **Pro lists:** "Premium financial metrics", "Advanced screening & alerts", "Priority support", "Portfolio tracking".
- **Elite lists:** "Custom screening tools", "Dedicated support", "Advanced portfolio tools", "White-label options".
- **The reality:** the site has no alerts (price alerts are still on the roadmap as not built), no white-label option and one support address.
- **The contradiction:** the FAQ (`faq.html` lines 325 and 355) says correctly that the plans differ only in how many analyses you can run a day.

A paying customer is promised things they won't get. That invites refund requests, and it is the kind of claim Australian consumer law treats as misleading.

**Fix:** give all three cards the same true list:
- the daily count
- every report, screener and signal
- CSV export
- the AI autopilot (Beta)
- cancel any time

Then add a nightly check so the cards can only name features from a short allowed list.

### 2. The home page's Pricing section has no way to buy for a signed-in member (sure) → proposal #10

- The section's only button is "Start Free Trial", which goes to `signup.html` for everyone (`index.html` line 888).
- A member whose trial has ended comes to the one place prices are shown and is sent to the sign-up form.
- There is no "buy" button anywhere in that section.

**Fix:** signed-in visitors see "Choose a plan", which goes to `dashboard.html#upgrade`. Signed-out visitors keep "Start Free Trial". The feature cards above it already switch buttons by who is looking (`only-signed-in` / `only-signed-out`), so this is a few lines.

### 3. The AI chat tells dashboard users the autopilot is paid-only (sure) → proposal #12

## What would bring sign-ups and payments

**Fix, at no cost:**

  - autopilot switched on

- the analytics tag written into every hand-edited page by `shared_parts.py`
- a nightly check that fails any indexed page without the tag

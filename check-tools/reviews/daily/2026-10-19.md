## Faults (most important first)

### 1. The news on stock pages gets wrong dates, and hundreds of pages are re-uploaded every day for nothing (sure)
- **Where:** `update_stock_news.py`, lines 951-979. When a stock's newest article is one the page already has, the script "refreshes" the page anyway, and line 970 changes **every** `data-timestamp` on the page to the newest article's date. That includes the older articles under "History".
- **Evidence on the live pages:**
  - `stocks/0006.HK.html`: an article that says "Thu, September 10" carries the timestamp of 16 Sep.
  - `stocks/ALAB.html`: an article of "Sat, October 10" carries 12 Oct, and its History button reads "October 12, 2026".
  - The browser rewrites the date from the timestamp, so visitors see the wrong date.
- **This is the cause of an open item in the notes** (section 11: "News data-timestamp values on stock pages get bumped past the article date … cause not found").
- **Why it matters for cost:** the script writes the page even when nothing changed, so S3 sees a newer file and uploads it. Today 1,184 stock pages were uploaded, but only 558 had a new data snapshot. Since 18 Oct news looks back 7 days, so more pages qualify each day. Those uploads are part of the $0.18 S3 bill.
- **A second, smaller fault in the same script:** the log always says "now 1 article". Line 1000 counts `<button onclick=`, but the History buttons are written as `<button data-timestamp=…` (line 791).
- **Fix:** delete the refresh block (keep the `continue`, so a page whose newest article is already there is left alone). Count the History buttons by `collapsed-btn`. Then correct the dates once from each article's own date text: the dates are still right in the visible "article-date" text.

### 2. On a phone, the dashboard's market-data button is hidden under the AI chat button (sure from the code; not seen on a real phone)
- **Where:** in `dashboard.html` (line 4351) the green market-data button sits at bottom 20px, right 20px, size 56px, layer 9999. Since 11 Oct the dashboard also loads `ai-chat.js`, whose blue button sits at bottom 20px, right 20px, size 60px, layer 10000.
- **What a visitor sees:** the blue button covers the green one completely, so on a phone the market data can only be opened by swiping.
- **On the home page** the same green button is moved up to 90px (`index.html`, lines 638 and 676). The dashboard never got that rule.
- **Why the test missed it:** `mobile_sidebar_check.js` opens the panel by calling `toggleMobileSidebar()` in code (line 139), not by tapping the button.
- **Fix:** give the dashboard the home page's `bottom: 90px` rule, and make the test tap the button where it sits on screen.

### 3. On a small phone, the AI chat window is taller than the screen (fairly sure)
- **Where:** `ai-chat.js`, line 19. On a phone the chat window is a fixed 500px tall and sits 80px above a button that is 20px from the bottom, so its top is 600px above the bottom of the screen.
- **What a visitor sees:** an iPhone SE shows about 550px of page in Safari, so the window's header and its × close button are off the top of the screen. With the keyboard open it is worse on every phone. This is a likely cause of the open item about the page jumping or zooming when the chat box is tapped.
- **Fix:** set the height to `min(500px, calc(100dvh - 120px))`, and check it in a phone-sized frame with the keyboard height taken off.

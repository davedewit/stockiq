## Faults

### 1. Stock news has not reached the news page since 24 Oct (sure)

- **What a visitor sees:** news.html's newest stock article is from 24 Oct 00:18 UTC. Today's run found about 300 new stock articles
  (30 batches of 10). They reached the stock pages and the home page's sidebar (news.js has 34 items from 25 and 26 Oct), but
  **not the news page**. Only the general market news there is fresh.
- **Why:** `update_stock_news.py` line 544 finds where to write by looking for `</div>` followed by `<footer`. Since the shared footer
  went in on 24 Oct (`shared_parts.py`), a comment line `<!-- SHARED_FOOTER_START ... -->` sits between them, so the pattern never
  matches. The script prints "❌ Could not find footer pattern in news.html" and gives up, **then prints "📝 Synced 10 articles to
  news.html" anyway**, which is untrue (30 times in today's log).
- `Sync_stock_to_news.py` line 139 (the manual repair tool) has the same pattern, so it cannot be used to repair it either.
  `update_news.py` and `remove_news_duplicates.py` look for the article-count block first, so they still work.
- **Why nothing caught it:** the news-freshness check added on 25 Oct (`site_audit.py` line 354) looks at the newest date of
  *any* article in news.html, and the general news is fresh. It never asks about stock news on its own. The daily email did flag
  the run (that is why it had a ⚠️), but no message on the Admin page says what it means.
- **The fix (small):** allow a comment between the two (`</div>\s*(?:<!--.*?-->\s*)*<footer`), or look for the article-count
  block first as `update_news.py` does, in both scripts. Print "Synced" only when the file was written. Run `Sync_stock_to_news.py`
  once to fill the gap back in. Make `news-stale` check the newest *stock* article in news.html separately. More generally: search
  every script for markup that `shared_parts.py` now rewrites (`<footer`, `<nav`), because this is the second script to rely on it.
- **Proposed on the Admin page** (below).

## Cost (the Admin page's two problems)

### 2. CloudFront charged $0.39 yesterday, after about $0 a day (fairly sure of the cause; a session can confirm it for free)

- AWS gives the first 1,000 cache-clearing paths of the month free, then charges $0.005 a path. $0.39 is exactly 78 paths over the
  limit. CloudFront's other charges (traffic, requests) are far inside their free allowance at this site's few visitors.
- Each one-off website deploy (`check-tools/web_deploy.sh` line 30 to 35) clears **one path per file**, and there were dozens of such
  deploys from 23 to 25 Oct, some with many files (the shared menu and footer went into every hand-edited page). The daily update
  clears `/*`, which counts as one path.
- So every further deploy this month costs half a cent a file, and the same will happen in any busy month. StockIQ's running cost is
  $1.23 this month against the $1 target; CloudFront is $0.46 of it.
- **The fix:** `web_deploy.sh` clears a single `/*` (one path) whenever a deploy has more than two or three files. To confirm the
  count first, free: `aws cloudfront list-invalidations --distribution-id EHXV50CPHY07R`, then each one's path count.
  `cost_check.py` could also warn when the month's paths pass about 800.
- **Proposed on the Admin page** (below).

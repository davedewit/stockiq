   "Data as of 6 Nov 2026 close" (`data-asof="2026-11-06"`): Friday. Monday 9 Nov was a full trading day in both

   "close" is the price part-way through the day. `update_stock_analysis.py` (line 236, `df.index[-1].date()`) takes whatever the
   last bar is and labels it the close; the log shows 3,280 of 3,467 symbols with prices and "Pages written: 929, unchanged: 2538"

   (Yahoo giving no Monday row to this Mac that night is the likeliest). `stale-stock-pages` in the site check only fires at more than 4
   days, so this passes today and would fire tomorrow if nothing changes.
   Effect: indexed pages (the part Google reads) show a score and a "close" that is either a day old or not a close; the sitemap
   dates follow it. Fix and the check that would catch it every day are in proposal 1 below.

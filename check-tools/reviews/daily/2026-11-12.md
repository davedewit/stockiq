## Numbers I doubted (3c). Today is Thursday 12 Nov, 06:10 UTC; the US market is shut, Asia is trading.
1. **"Behind the fund by $4,629" (opening check) against the day table above it.** The table's last close is $93,616 (11 Nov); the
   headline account is $92,961, $655 lower. Not a fault: the account is marked live (Taiwan, China, Hong Kong, Japan, Australia are
   open and fell overnight, about a third of the holdings by currency) while the fund is marked at Wednesday's US close. It reads as
   "behind by $4,629" now and would read about $4,000 at the same moment on both sides. Worth one clause in the opening check and on the
   Practice tab when a market is open and the fund's is not: "account live, fund at last close". Sure about the cause (the currency list
   of the holdings and the time of day), not about whether the tab already says so.
2. **Sector funds' calls "19 of 34 right".** 19/34 is 56%; a coin flip gives that or better about three times in ten. And the calls
   overlap (each is judged over a week, a call every trading day), so 34 calls carry roughly seven independent weeks. The experiment
   `theme-sleeve` said the calls must be "right more often than chance from 30 scored calls before it may act". 30 is passed and the
   record is **not** beyond chance (it was 3 of 8 on 30 Oct, so it has moved toward chance, not away). Verdict for the register: keep
   them recorded, never acted on; judge again at 60 with the overlap allowed for. No cost to keep (about 2 US cents a month).
3. **"Updated daily" on stock pages** was already queried by the review of 10 Nov (#24 waits). Today AAPL says `data-asof="2026-11-11"`,
   which is Wednesday's close and right for a Thursday morning in Sydney/UTC before the US opens. The site check's one stale page
   (ROKU, 6 Nov) is a single share; not a pattern today.

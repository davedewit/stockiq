## Faults (first)

### 1. The weekly review treats the day a share was bought as the day it was sold (sure; proposal #45)
- `check-tools/trade_causes.py` line 48 picks "finished trades of the last N days" by the field `t`, and lines 62-67 use it as the sale
  time. In the autopilot's record `t` is the **buy** time (function line 2001); the sale time is `sold` (line 2062).
- **Evidence:** today's run says "finished in the period: 30 trades" in part 2 and "Finished trades of the last 7 days: 2" in part 4b.
  It says MGLU3.SA was "sold 23 Nov"; it was bought then and sold about 26 Nov. The saved reviews show the same gap: 30 Oct 55 against
  5; 6 Nov 52 against 7; 12 Nov 37 against 10; 20 Nov 32 against 5 (8 days).
- **What is wrong as a result:** which trades are looked at (only those bought and sold inside the window), and every "its market / the
  share" cause and "against its own index" figure, which measure the market over the days **before** the buy. The 20 Nov line "the picks
  did -13.43% a trade against their own markets: clearly behind" cannot be relied on, nor can the 23 Oct conclusion in the notes ("35 of
  48 losses the share alone, picks +0.21%"): the line has been there since the first version. Question 55 leans on this script.
- **Change:** use `sold` for the filter, the one-event grouping and the index window; rerun over all trades; correct the notes. **Check
  that would catch it:** the review stops if part 4b's count differs from part 2's.

### 2. A typed company name spends the free analysis and shows "API Error: 404" (sure from the code, not tried live; proposal #46)
- The home page box says "Search for tickers or companies". The button sends whatever was typed, in capitals (`index.html` 1121-1135),
  and the analysis page runs it at once (`analysis-functions.js` 2879-2888).
- The use is **counted first** (lines 965-968), then the report is asked (1069). "APPLE", "TESLA" or "MICROSOFT" are not tickers, so
  for a symbol the price source does not know the
  function answers 404 (line 604) and the page shows "❌ ERROR: API Error: 404. Please try again or contact support" (1419), then
  the sign-up pop-up. A visitor who is not signed in has one a day: it is gone, with nothing to show.
- The suggestion list would save them, but it appears only after the lookup has tried 20 exchanges one after another
  (`validate_symbol.py`), so it can take seconds; pressing Enter first is enough to hit this.
- **Change:** count a use only when a report comes back; when nothing is found, say so plainly and offer the matching symbols.
  **Check:** a made-up symbol in `anon_run.js` must leave the count unchanged.

What is a Stock Screener?

A stock screener is a powerful tool that filters thousands of stocks based on specific criteria you set. Instead of manually researching every stock, screeners help you quickly identify investment opportunities that match your strategy.

🎯 Why Use Stock Screeners?

  • Save Time: Filter 8,000+ stocks in seconds instead of hours
  • Find Opportunities: Discover undervalued or high-growth stocks
  • Reduce Risk: Avoid stocks with poor fundamentals
  • Stay Disciplined: Stick to your investment criteria

Essential Screening Criteria

📊 Fundamental Filters

  • Market Cap ($1B+ for stability)
  • P/E Ratio (5-25 for value)
  • Revenue Growth (>10% annually)
  • Debt-to-Equity (<0.5 for safety)
  • ROE (>15% for profitability)

📈 Technical Filters

  • Price above 50-day MA
  • RSI between 30-70
  • Volume above average
  • 52-week high proximity
  • Positive price momentum

Proven Screening Strategies

💎 Value Investing Screen

Goal: Find undervalued companies trading below intrinsic value

  • P/E Ratio: 5-15
  • P/B Ratio: <2.0
  • Debt-to-Equity: <0.3
  • ROE: >10%
  • Dividend Yield: >2%

🚀 Growth Stock Screen

Goal: Identify companies with strong growth potential

  • Revenue Growth: >20% annually
  • Earnings Growth: >15% annually
  • P/E Ratio: 15-40
  • ROE: >20%
  • Market Cap: >$1B

💰 Dividend Income Screen

Goal: Find reliable dividend-paying stocks for income

  • Dividend Yield: 3-8%
  • Payout Ratio: <60%
  • 5-year dividend growth: >5%
  • Debt-to-Equity: <0.5
  • Free Cash Flow: Positive

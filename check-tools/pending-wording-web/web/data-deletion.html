Data Deletion Instructions

If you have used Facebook Login to access StockIQ and want to delete your data, you can request deletion through the following methods:

Method 1: Facebook Settings

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings & Privacy
  2. Click on "Settings"
  3. Select "Apps and Websites"
  4. Find "StockIQ" in your app list
  5. Click "Remove" to revoke access and delete associated data

Method 2: Direct Request

Email us directly:

Send a data deletion request to: privacy@stockiq.tech

Include in your email:

  • Your Facebook email address
  • Subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
  • Confirmation that you want all your StockIQ data deleted

What Data Gets Deleted

When you request data deletion, we will remove:

  • Your account information (name, email)
  • Login history and session data
  • Usage analytics and preferences
  • Any stored analysis requests or results

Processing Time

Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been permanently deleted from our systems.

Questions?

If you have any questions about data deletion or need assistance, contact us at privacy@stockiq.tech
